# Lessons from client notes

Each note below changed a rule in `SKILL.md`; the section it led to is in brackets. The films are the teaser (a 60 s teaser for an executive education program), the opener (a company's internal event opener, six versions) and the reel (this project's own 60 s homepage film). The notes are paraphrased and shortened. Read them when a new note arrives. Diagnose with measurement, fix the cause, and report before and after numbers.

## From the teaser

- **Flat on "you".** The take ran into the word and dropped it 6 dB. Re-directed and measured, it went from 0.08 s, +0.8 st, -6.0 dB to 0.38 s, +5.3 st, +4.2 dB. [5.3]
- **It repeats the people at the end.** The closing montage was cut, and its five seconds became the payoff the opening had set up. [3.2, 8.1]

## From the opener

- **The voice should sound like the audience.** Recast in the audience's own accent. [3.4]
- **The experts are not highlighted enough.** A 2.3 s name ripple became a spotlight tour, two beats a person. [3.7]
- **The joke does not need to be said out loud.** The joke now plays in type, in silence. [2]
- **The words flash so quickly they are missed.** One moment per screen at 78 to 84 px, spoken by the narrator and held while she speaks. [3.2]
- **Use icons to show people coming together.** The template's people icons gather into one crowd, then one ring; only the named people kept their photos. [3.7]
- **Open on leadership's frame, then zoom into the team.** The leader's frame first, the phrases held without quote marks, then a dive into the team's dot. [2]
- **Turn the campaign image into a fast building animation.** The graphic was taken apart and built on the beat. It enters through the headline's full stop and leaves as the ring's dot. [3.8]
- **Some pictures are low resolution, the zooms are not smooth, and a word on screen differs from the script.** These were all notes on a run that had no eyes. The photo rule, the layout audit and the motion audit now catch them before the client does. [3.7, 6.4, 8.3]
- **Less slide template, more of the bright colors.** Each moment became a full field of its template color, opening from where its number sits, and the template's corner furniture went. [3.9]
- **Weave the opening back in, and bring back the leads.** The score grew by whole bars, every line was re-placed by rule, and the leads got a tour of two beats each. [3.7, 5.2, 8.5]
- **Weave in the team's slogan.** Every spoken take breathed out its last word, so the slogan plays in type, on the music. [3.1]

## From the reel

- **A different male voice would be better.** Three voices were cast on the turn line by measurement, auditioned in context, and the pick voiced the script. The next note: none of them is dramatic. An alto the client had approved on an earlier film read the whole script in one pass, and the note after that kept the first narrator. Measurement rejects bad takes; the client's ear chooses. Check the speech provider's credit balance before a full re-record: the last recast got two of its four takes. [3.4]

## What the audit found after the eyes had looked

On the opener's v5, after five rounds of looking, the layout audit still found a person icon crossing the title, two phrases overlapping at a handover, and a four-word hint card on screen for 0.9 s. On v6, teaching the audit about masks, clips and covers took it from 49 false issues to none, so the real ones stood out. [6.4]
